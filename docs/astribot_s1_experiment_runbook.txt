8. THEO DOI KHI INFER
---------------------

Moi cycle in:
  inference=<seconds>
  raw_arm_jump=<radian>
  grippers=[left, right]

Tracking command-versus-measured-state:
  OK:       max error <= 0.15 rad
  WARNING:  max error > 0.15 rad
  CRITICAL: max error > 0.30 rad

Sau 3 CRITICAL lien tiep, runtime dung truoc command ke tiep. Khi thoat sau khi
da command, runtime publish hold bang command da smooth gan nhat.

Xem nhanh log:

  grep -E 'WARNING|CRITICAL|stale|latency|inference=|raw_arm_jump' \
    "$LOG_DIR"/*.log

Neu co bat ky dau hieu sau, bam E-stop ngay:
- rung/manh bat thuong;
- di sai phia hoac roi khoi workspace;
- va cham ban/do vat/than robot;
- tracking error tang lien tuc;
- camera bi dung/che trong khi robot dang chay.

Luu y: runtime Pelican hien CHUA tu dong record video. Neu can bang chung thi
record camera bang cong cu ROS/bridge rieng truoc khi execute.
